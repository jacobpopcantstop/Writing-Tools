Sketch Comedy Draft Feedback

Instructor Evaluation & Notes

1. The Core

Is the base reality established quickly and clearly within the first few pages?

What is the central unusual thing? Is the comedic pattern clear, focused, and easy to follow?

2. Structure & Escalation

Are the comedic beats distinct? Do they hit at the right times without dragging?

Does the sketch escalate properly? Does the game get bigger or raise the stakes with each beat?

Does the sketch end on a strong, definitive laugh or a sharp conceptual out?

3. Characters & Dialogue

Do characters have clear perspectives? Is the straight man/absurd character dynamic working effectively?

Is the dialogue tight? Are there unnecessary lines getting in the way of jokes?

4. Mechanics & Formatting

Does it follow standard formatting rules? Are stage directions clear and easy to shoot/stage?

5. Overall Impressions

Highlight the strongest joke, character, or concept.

Identify the main roadblock preventing the sketch's full potential.

6. Action Items for Next Draft
